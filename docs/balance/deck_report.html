Summary — per subject

One block per deck under test. Every number is pooled across that subject's matchups in this run — win rate is from the subject's own side, so a deck that sat on the enemy side of a batch still reads its own result. Damage per turn counts every point of HP the subject removed, however it got there, which is why it can exceed the sum of the per-card direct-damage column below.

Card leaderboard

Sortable and filterable. Static score is what powerscale prices the card at from its text; measured score replaces only its damage and status terms with what the card actually did. A wide Δ means the card performs differently than its text implies, not that the two formulas disagree. Dead rate counts copies that reached hand and were never played (the same convention as the deck-level ratio); play rate counts casts per time the card entered hand, which is a different question. Rows built on fewer than 20 plays are muted. Click a card name for its appendix.

Matchup drill-down

One row per subject per matchup. Rows built on fewer iterations than the run's confidence floor are badged low sample — a thin sample is a confidence flag, never a balance finding. Click a row to expand its turn order, damage and per-matchup top cards and statuses; usage shifts by opponent, so those are worth reading per row rather than only in aggregate.

Redlines

CARD_OVER_BUDGET rows are, carried verbatim from the committed balance_redlines.csv. The three new kinds proposed in the design doc are shown with example rows so you can react to the severity coloring and grouping before the thresholds are locked in.

Archetype & card appendix

One block per subject — OS firmware description + authored archetype summary + full card grid. Meant to make the standing "every card/deck needs an appendix" report rule automatic instead of hand-typed. The OS text is pulled from the firmware registry at generation time; the archetype summary is authored (design intent is not derivable from card data) and a subject with none is flagged by the generator rather than shipping blank.
