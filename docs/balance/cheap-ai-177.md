## What was measured

The cheap AI is a list of named features with a weight each. For every legal action it simulates the action once, adds up weight times feature, and plays the best one. It was fitted to copy the full AI (the shipped search AI) from a recording of the full AI playing itself. The bar from the ticket (C2) is two things at once: its win rate against the full AI is at least the `lite` tier's win rate against the full AI on the same games, and it is at least 10 times faster per decision than `lite`. Nothing was tuned toward the bar. If it is missed, it is reported as missed.

"Class-averaged" below means the 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 win rates are averaged with equal weight, because most games are 1v1 and a pooled number would be a 1v1 number. "10 times faster" has to hold at both 1v1 and 3v3.

## 6. If it missed: what I would add next

Of 420 decisions where cheap disagreed with the full AI: cheap stopped when the full AI kept playing 1%, cheap kept playing when the full AI stopped 5%, and both played but chose different actions 94%.

The features the teacher's choice differed from cheap's on most, in order: allyDeaths (the teacher's pick had less of it), evalDelta (the teacher's pick had less of it), cardsDrawn (the teacher's pick had less of it).

The biggest block is two plays that both look fine one move deep. What the full AI sees that a one-move policy cannot is the REST OF THE TURN: what the remaining hand can still do with the energy left after this play. The feature I would add next is `handPotential`: the best damage (and kills) the cards still in hand could add this turn from the position after the play, one reducer call per remaining card. It is the cheapest way to give a one-move policy a view of sequencing.

This is a suggestion from where the disagreements are, not something tried. Nothing in this report was tuned toward the bar.

## Decisions and next steps

1. The cheap AI misses the bar as defined above. It stays an option for simulations (`playerAiTier` / `enemyAiTier` = `cheap`) and nothing in the shipped game or the existing balance runs uses it. Say whether to try the next feature.
2. Shipped enemies do not use it (C3). Whether wild enemies should is a later ruling, and these are the numbers to make it from.
3. The weights are a fit to a recording of the full AI. Re-record and re-fit whenever the full AI's evaluation changes (`runRecordTeacher.ts`, then `runFitWeights.ts`), then re-run this measurement.
