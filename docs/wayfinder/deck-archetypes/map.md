# Deck Archetypes — Wayfinder Map

Label: `wayfinder:map`

**Tracker conventions (local-markdown fallback):** tickets are files in [`tickets/`](tickets/). Each carries `Type` (`wayfinder:research|prototype|grilling|task`), `Status` (`open|closed`), `Assignee` (blank = unclaimed), and `Blocked by` (links). A session claims a ticket by writing its name into `Assignee` before working. The **frontier** is every ticket that is open, unblocked (all Blocked-by closed), and unclaimed. Resolutions are recorded in the ticket's `## Resolution` section on close, and gisted below.

## Destination

Every species ships **per-OS starting decks** — 32 decks across 16 species — each deliberately built so its firmware's mechanic matters in play, every card priced inside the rev-3 power curve (`docs/power_curve_spec.md`), validated by `npm run balance` (with team-battle scenarios where a hook needs allies), and landed in the game's data model as player-facing content. Done when all 32 decks are in the registry, the §2.3 OS Variance Audit measures real firmware gaps (no matchup where one side's hook cannot fire), and the OS-gap redline list reflects firmware, not deck fill.

## Notes

- **Execution override:** like the debug-toolkit map, this map carries implementation — build tickets graduate out of the fog as design tickets resolve.
- **Scope, locked in the charting grilling (2026-08-05, Henry):** all 16 species get the treatment (the three starters' decks are seeds, not sacred — Henry rates fenrir, kraken, maybe ratatoskr and fafnir as the only solid decks today, and even those can be reworked so the 32 land well together). New cards are freely in scope — the thin element pools (Earth/Ice/Light 9 cards, Air 10, Dark 11 vs Fire 20/Nature 20/Water 21) make that unavoidable — but every new or changed card lands on the rev-3 curve (`50×E−10`, the status price table, `BUDGET_BANDS {0:1.0, 1:4.0, 2:9.0, 3+:14.0}` in `src/debug/balance/powerscale.ts`) and is graded by `npm run balance`. Hooks that are structurally dead in 1v1 get **team-battle scenarios**, not redesign-by-default — after [the OS design review](tickets/09-os-design-review.md)'s reworks, the team-scenario list is valkyrie_v1 (EINHERJAR_RALLY became the `einherjar_standard` team daemon card per [the rework specs](tickets/11-os-rework-specs.md)); nidhoggr_v2 and draugr_v2 are rebuilt solo-live.
- **Why this map exists — the confound, with numbers:** `osVarianceScenario()` hands the same shared `baseDeck` to both firmware variants, and the audit found the current §2.3 redlines partly measure dead hooks, not power. kraken_v2 needs a 3e+ Water card (deck max cost: 2 — hook never fires) yet "wins 100%"; hraesvelgr_v1 needs a discard outlet (**zero exist in the whole 111-card pool**) yet v2 "wins 100%"; skoll_v2 is dead in its deck (its only Burn is self-targeted) while skoll_v1 turned out to work in 1v1, so skoll's clean §2.3 pass measures one live hook against one dead one; nidhoggr_v2's on-faint hook can't matter in 1v1 yet shows a 40% gap. Post-rework baseline run (2026-08-05, registry `1:e900e03a` after the ticket-13 wiring — numbers unchanged from `1:5e763093`): 9 species over the 15% cap (valkyrie's new 19% gap is the first *real* measurement of its pair), kraken/gullinbursti at ~100%, sleipnir at 99%, FTK cleared. All remaining gaps are deck problems — no firmware defects, no dead hooks except deck-starved ones.
- `docs/NorseExpansion_Full.md` is **inspiration only**: it predates the registry (its Jormungandr archetypes are stun/energy-denial Water, the registry's jormungandr plays Poison; its Surtr slot is now fenrir), and many of its card ideas need mechanics the engine doesn't have (Scrap economy, energy transfer, Ice-vulnerability, revive, reflection). The real vocabulary is `ActionType`/`StatusType` in `src/engine/types.ts`; the [firmware audit](tickets/01-firmware-truth-and-enabler-audit.md) resolution holds the per-archetype keep/adapt/reject table. Anything needing new engine work surfaces as its own decision, never assumed.
- **Design principles (locked in the OS design review, 2026-08-05):** (1) *team/solo is a deliberate axis* — the roster should host OSes that promote multi-party play and OSes that excel solo, but no species carries two team-dependent OSes (valkyrie: v1 team + new v2 solo). (2) *Archetype uniqueness at the payoff level* — species may share enabler cards (even cross-element, e.g. a discard package), but no two mingmings run the same archetype; payoffs must differ (ramp vs control). (3) *Tuning numbers move in 5s* — Henry dislikes 13s and 14s; card numbers land on 5-steps (locked during the jormungandr pass). (4) *A card that does nothing for an OS should do something ELSE, never nothing* — locked 2026-08-07 with hel_v2, whose UNDERWORLD_GATEWAY makes Energy a dead stat. The collection is shared across all 16 species, so a card dead in one OS is a trap the player learns by wasting a pick, not a neutral non-choice. Any OS that changes or removes a resource must name what the orphaned resource becomes — see [Resource conversion](tickets/37-resource-conversion.md).
- **Cross-map:** `docs/wayfinder/power-curve-rebalance/tickets/` 01 (mirror stalemates), 02 (OS variance gaps) and 03 (budget overages) are downstream of this map — deck rebuilds re-baseline their numbers. Don't re-derive their findings; don't fix them there while decks are in flight here.

Repo rules: `npx vitest run` + `npx tsc -b` + `npx vite build` before shipping; for balance/deck changes also `npm run balance` and read the `docs/balance/balance_report.json` diff.
