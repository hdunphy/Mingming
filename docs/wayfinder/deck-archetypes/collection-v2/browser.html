v2.1 · 2026-09-23

Henry’s review, applied

Two rules now govern every number: the slot tax (a card also costs the card it displaces — 0e ≈ 12, 1e ≈ 30, 2e ≥ 70, 3e ≥ 120, and a 2e/3e card must do what two 1e cards cannot) and the one-job rule at 0e (no 0e card is another 0e card plus a rider).

  • Draw glue — Quick Scan removed; Forage (draw 1, take 15 power) is the generic 0e draw in every kit. Undertow = draw + 1 Weakened on yourself (Legion tested) and stays Water for OUROBOROS.
  • Wording — Capacitor and Tidal Battery say Energized. Tidal Battery unchanged otherwise (every ally gains 1 Energized; run card).
  • Fenrir self damage — Desperate Strike (8p, +1 Str, −3% HP) and Glass Cannon (45p, −5% HP) join the kit; Fury Strike and Blood Rite move to the run pool.
  • 2e / 3e rebands — Hydro Blast 120 · Maelstrom 100 + 3 Dazed · Tidal Wave 55 side · Boiling Surge 55 + 2 Burn · Pile On 35, again if Dazed · Crippling Vine is now a SIDE card (20 to each, 2 Weakened each) · Seed Bomb 20/card · Crushing Depths 20/stack · Slander 15/stack · Cinder Lance 40 + 6/Sharp · Sharp Edge 15/stack · Bark Smash 6/point · Venom Glut 15/stack consumed · Blightbloom 30 + 5 Poison · Ink Cloud 25 side · Pack Tactics 15×3 · Wildfire 45 side.
  • 1e rebands — Surge Protection → 1e, 25p + refund · Venom Fang 30 · Brand 25 · Snap 20/+12 · Slag Strike 22 · Riptide Run 20 · Sap Strength 20 + 6/Weakened.
  • Multi-hit rule — A multi-hit card carries the full band split across its hits — Flare Burst 15×2, Pack Tactics 23×3, Serpent Flurry 10×3, Acorn Toss 6×2. Ragnarok Edge +1 power per 1% HP missing (max 50). Flashover 50 + 15/Burn (80 at 2 Burn). Scald 2 Burn + 1 self Dazed. Pile On 45, again if Dazed.
  • 0e one-job — Ember Jab = the Burn HIT (8p + 1 Burn); Ignite = the Burn CANTRIP (1 Burn, draw if already Burning). Snarl = 2 Weakened on one; Pollen Cloud = 1 Weakened on the side. Acorn Toss = 5p twice (the 0e multi-hit; Scurry removed); Tackle = the flat 12.
