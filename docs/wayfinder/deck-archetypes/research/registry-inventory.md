# Registry inventory — every card in no deck

*Ticket 55 Part 3 deliverable. Read at registry `1:b66be225`, after the jormungandr rebuild.*

**NOTHING HAS BEEN DELETED.** This is a list for Henry to review; deletion is its own follow-up
ticket. Generated by walking all 32 OS deck lists plus the control, then subtracting.

**53 cards sit in no deck.** The registry holds 213 cards, so that is **24%** of the
pool. Excluded from the count and listed separately at the bottom: tokens (`isToken`),
hook- and card-generated cards, and the two known intentional keeps.

## How to read this

A card in no deck is not automatically dead weight. Three different things are mixed in here:

- **Draft/reward pool material.** The collection is shared across all 16 species, so a card
  nobody's *starting* deck runs is still something a player can pick up mid-run. Most of this
  list is that.
- **Retired poke twins.** `frost_jab`, `rock_throw` and friends were deliberately retired into
  `water_slap` (Tackle) by the deck rulebook, ticket 04. They are supposed to be here.
- **Genuinely orphaned mechanics** — cards whose enabler never shipped, or whose archetype
  moved. These are the ones worth a decision.

## Orphaned by this ticket

Two cards left a deck for the first time in ticket 55 and landed here:

- **`poison_injection`** (0e, 1 Poison) and **`acid_splash`** (1e, 1 Poison + 1 Dazed) were
  jormungandr_v2's attrition core. TOXIN_FANG_OS wants a *pile*, not a trickle, so both were
  cut for `venom_fang` and `water_slap`. `poison_injection` scores **0.3 against a 1.0 band** —
  it is the lowest-scoring non-token card in the registry and the strongest deletion candidate
  on this list.
- **`capacitor`** is NOT on this list — it is still in kraken_v2 — but ticket 55 removed it from
  jormungandr_v2 and named it a ramp draft card. Recorded here so the next reader does not
  re-derive that.

`shatter` is the one to read twice: ticket 50 proved it is **structurally dead** (a Stun applied
on turn T is gone before the applier's turn T+1, because `StunnedBehavior.endTurn` always returns
`null`), and kept it as a drop-only card. `entangle` and `stunning_strike` above carry the same
mechanic and have never been re-checked against that finding.
