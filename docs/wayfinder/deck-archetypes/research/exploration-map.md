# Exploration map - session rulings (Henry, 2026-08-20 car session)

## Ruled

- **A run = THREE BIOMES, each a mix of TWO elements.** Starting a run offers a choice of
  THREE gyms/bosses.
- **The gym is a GAUNTLET: three fights, NO healing between them.** The boss's team draws
  ONE mingming from each of the run's biomes - the run trains you for its own final exam.
- **FULL HEAL between regular nodes** (tuned fights end winners at ~20 HP - carry-over
  would death-spiral). The run-level HP economy lives INSIDE the gauntlet: sustain decks,
  potions, blood costs all matter across the three unhealed fights.
- **Nodes:** wild encounters, elites, marketplace (buy cards), events, workshops
  (assemble/reflash), the gym. **Potions + relics exist as run-boost systems** (design at
  the economy session).
- **Visibility: types visible, contents hidden** - 'fire encounter / fire-and-earth
  encounter / event / marketplace / elite / boss' readable from the map.
- **Map shape: an explorable GRAPH, explicitly NOT Spire's three lanes** - you find your
  way to the boss, with room to FARM if you don't feel ready. (This is the staged
  overworld: an explorable graph with farming IS the walkable world minus the walking.)
- **Length: 35-45 minutes; 8-10 battles plus the gauntlet = 10-13 fights total.**
- **Encounter size: SYMMETRIC BY DEFAULT, ASYMMETRIC AS CONTENT.** Enemy parties match
  yours (recruiting raises the opposition - power growth self-balances). Designed,
  map-visible exceptions: the ALPHA (one overtuned elite-frame wild vs your full team,
  guards a rare blueprint) and the AMBUSH (their 3 vs your 2, marked high-risk node).
  Gauntlet is always full 3v3 curated.
- **Run difficulty = TIERS, not scaling** (leveling is removed; player power is
  horizontal, so difficulty is a content ladder): gym difficulty ratings, harder tiers
  unlock by beating gyms - meaner curated teams, more elites, enemy relics; never bigger
  numbers on the same enemies. Long tail: opt-in run modifiers (ascension-shaped).
