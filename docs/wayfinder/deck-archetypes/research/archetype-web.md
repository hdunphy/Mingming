# The archetype web - neutral-matchup texture by design (Henry, 2026-08-16)

- Status: adopted alongside the 3v3 decision. This is a DESIGN INPUT for every remaining
  pass: each deck gets a declared prey and predator. Neutral lopsidedness must be a WEB
  statement, never a power accident (the gullinbursti_v1 pathology: 54% mean, 11 blowouts).

## The wheel

**ZOO/VELOCITY preys on RAMP/SUSTAIN preys on CONTROL/ATTRITION preys on ZOO.**
BURST/BERSERK is the flex spoke - punishes sloppy setup from any role.
Soft counters (roughly 65-80% cells), not hard - hard counters live in the TYPE chart.

## Orphan roles assigned (Henry + designer session, 2026-08-16)

- **fafnir_v1 HOARD_PROTOCOL = RAMP/SUSTAIN - the roster's second 0-RAMP-PREMIUM patient.**
  He banks Energy as Energized at 1% maxHp interest per point per turn - a skip-spend that
  pays to WAIT, so the >2x premium law applies verbatim. At 32.0 field / 10 violations his
  premium is presumably missing like capacitor's was. His pass = the kraken-72 template
  (prey: control; predator: zoo; peers: valkyrie/audhumbla/kraken_v2), GATED on ticket 72's
  learnings - pre-staged as ticket 74 when 72 ships.
- **fafnir_v2 CORRUPTED_GOLD = ANTI-CONTROL BRUISER.** He converts debuffs into Strengthened
  and sheds a stack of each - he WANTS to be debuffed, which makes him a designed predator
  of the debuff-control decks (ratatoskr/draugr/huldra). His prey cells get the 65-80
  license; everything else 10-90.
- **gullinbursti_v1 + v2 = BURST/BERSERK (flex).** The flex role carries NO prey/predator
  license: burst decks are held to 10-90 in EVERY neutral cell - broadly fair spoilers.
  gullinbursti_v1's 11 blowouts at a 54% mean are therefore ALL bugs (binary
  prime-hits-or-whiffs variance, most likely) - queue his flattening pass after hraesvelgr.

## How it gates

Under the bucket-band standard: a deck may run 65-80% into its prey role and 20-35% into
its predator role IN NEUTRAL TYPE cells - that is the designed texture. Outside those
role relationships, neutral cells target 10-90 (hard floor: no absolute 0/100). In 3v3
the two wheels (type chart + this web) are the team-building space.
