# Economy session rulings (Henry, 2026-08-20, running notes - bites land as decided)

- **Deploy choice: SIMPLE - party size is party size.** No partial deployment; focused
  hunting is served by alpha/rare-blueprint nodes, not smaller fights. (Closes the
  exploration-map open item.)
- **SCRAP IS RUN-SCOPED (Henry's call, anti-mudflation):** earned in-run by winning
  battles and SELLING cards to the marketplace; spent on marketplace cards, potions,
  relics, and card REMOVAL (designer-added sink, valkyrie's thinning lesson). Resets with
  the run. Closed-loop = tunable per-run like a deck.
- **BLUEPRINTS ARE THE ONLY PERSISTENT CURRENCY:** assembly (ranch AND workshop) costs
  blueprints only; reflash costs a blueprint; dead runs pay out in blueprints.
- Flagged, not needed now: no persistent currency exists for future ranch
  upgrades/cosmetics - invent a badge/trophy meta-currency only if that need appears.

## Bite two RULED (Henry, 2026-08-20): cards are RUN-SCOPED - in-run deckbuilding is the second progression track

- **Cards do NOT persist.** Every run starts fresh with a PARTIAL deck: the starter brings
  ~a third of its kit (generic hits + a signature, Spire-strike-shaped start). Each
  recruit brings a similar fraction. The run BUILDS toward the ~20-25 cards a good 3v3
  deck wants via post-fight picks (1-of-3), marketplace purchases, events, and removal.
  Math checks WITHOUT adding fights: ~15 cards from team-building + picks/purchases -
  removals = 20-25 by the gauntlet. THE TEAM IS THE DECK.
- **Card collection = a CODEX** (seen/played cards logged; completion pays cosmetics or
  blueprints) - collection as achievement layer, ZERO power attached.
- OPEN (last economy item): reward-pool source - designer recommends **picks draw from the
  CURRENT PARTY'S species pools** (recruiting = choosing your draft pool; mono vs spread
  teams draft different runs; optional off-pool wild-cards at events). Henry deciding.

## Bites three + four RULED (Henry, 2026-08-20)

- **Single-use slots (3): Spire-consumable-shaped, own twist, NOT called potions** -

- **Party-wide passives from elites, NOT called relics** - 'a weaker OS for the whole

  reduced %, or an item that returns at % - DEFERRED TO PLAYTESTING deliberately.
