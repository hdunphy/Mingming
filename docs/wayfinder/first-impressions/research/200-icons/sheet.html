Needs a ruling
Eliteicons.ts says helmet (ruled); the map's own glyph set (nodeGlyphs.ts) is proposed as star. One kind of node, two icons. Use helmet on the map too, or keep star for the gate?
Crownalpha (icons.ts) and the map's gym share crown. icons.ts's gym is building-bank, so the gym is a bank in one place and a crown in the other.
SkullPoison is skull (ruled) and the chrome skull is skull too. The chrome one only shows on the run summary's lost line, Poison only on battle chips, so they never share a screen. Probably fine.
Swordsword is the wild node, the map's fight node and the attack stat.
Houseshome (ranch) and building-cottage (town) are both small houses. Distinct at 20px? See page 2 and 3.
Rivalswords is crossed blades. The old icon comment says crossed swords read as a close button at 18px on the map. Look at it at 16px on page 3.
HelmetRuled, but at 16px (page 3) the Tabler helmet reads as a rounded blob more than a helmet. At 24px it is fine. If it is too vague on the map, I can show shield-half or chess-knight beside it on a second sheet.
Stroke weightStatuses draw at 2.5, map glyphs at 2.4, icons.ts at 1.7. The ticket says one shared 1.7. Tabler's own default is 2. That is a 200c call; the sheet shows each surface at its current weight.
