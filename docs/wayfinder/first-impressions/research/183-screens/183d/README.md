# 183d screenshots: the console and the toast

The Fire battle with the player inside a run, which is what draws the macro rack and the tip toast.

- `console-*` the bottom of the battle: two macro chips (one held and ready, yellow; one held but
  blocked until a target is picked, dim; one empty slot, dashed), the draw pile as three navy backs
  with a yellow count, the hand, the discard as a dashed slot with a white count, and the 200 x 50
  yellow End Turn. The toast above the console is the tip: a navy slanted bar, one sentence, no buttons.
- `end-turn-nudge-*` End Turn pressed with Energy left: the cards that could still be played wear the
  amber ring (the button flashes a white ring three times; a yellow ring on a yellow button is nothing).
- Both sizes are 1280x800 and 1920x1080 (D7).

To retake them: `npm run dev`, open `/Mingming/stage.html?biome=Fire&run=1` (dev only).

The "Show tips" switch is in Settings beside "Show advanced content"; it is on screen in the 183f
Settings screenshots.
