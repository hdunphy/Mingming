## Runes (the six Instinct modifiers)

The six Runes are Elder Futhark rune names, which fits the word "Rune". The "Why" column gives each rune's real meaning.

| Now | Proposed | Why | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amplifier | **Fehu** | wealth, cattle: more of what you have | Your Instinct's number goes up: one more stack, or half again as much. |
| Repeater | **Jera** | the turning year: the cycle comes round again | An Instinct that holds itself to once a turn gets one more. |
| Relay | **Mannaz** | the people: your whole side, not just you | An Instinct that only watches you now watches your whole side. |
| Splitter | **Gebo** | the gift: what you get, the rest of your side gets | What your Instinct gives you, the rest of your side gets too. |
| Overclock | **Uruz** | the aurochs, raw strength | Every stack you hold counts for one more when a card cashes it. |
| Failsafe | **Algiz** | protection | Whatever your Instinct costs you, it stops costing. |

A player will not know what Fehu means, but the Rune's own text says what it does and the name is a label, like "Venomfang". If you would rather have plain English (for example "Boon" for Gebo), say so for any row.
