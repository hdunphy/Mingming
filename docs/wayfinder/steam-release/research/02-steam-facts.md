# Steam facts the roadmap depends on (verified 2026-08-21)

Re-verify each before acting on it — Valve changes these; the Steamworks documentation is the source of truth.

- **Steam Direct fee:** $100 USD per app, non-refundable, recoupable after the app reaches $1,000 adjusted gross revenue; only partner-account Admins can buy app credits; identity + tax interview required. No 30-day waiting period is stated on the fee page itself — check the release-process page for the current first-release waiting rule and the store-page-must-be-live-first rule at the time of ticket 41. Source: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee
- **Generative-AI disclosure:** mandatory for AI-generated content that appears in the game or in associated material (store page, marketing assets) — a free-text disclosure of how it was used; a separate checkbox if the game generates AI content at runtime. AI coding/workflow tools used during development are exempt. Sources: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/valve-tweaks-and-clarifies-ai-disclosure-rules-for-steam, https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/steam-ai-disclosure-rules-2026-indie-developer-guide
- **Steam Next Fest:** one participation per game, ever; the game must be unreleased (an already-launched Early Access title is ineligible); a public Coming Soon page and a live demo are required; registration closes ~7–8 weeks before the fest. 2026 fests: Feb 23–Mar 2, Jun 15–22, Oct 19–26. 2027 dates unpublished as of today. Sources: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest/2026october, https://presskit.gg/field-guides/next-fest-scheduling-registration
- **Wrapping a web game:** Electron and NW.js are the wrappers the Steam-binding libraries officially support; `steamworks.js` is the maintained TypeScript binding (achievements, overlay, cloud); Tauri gives small binaries via OS webviews but weaker graphics and no first-class Steamworks binding. Sources: https://www.webgamedev.com/publishing/desktop, https://github.com/ceifa/steamworks.js/
- **Review threshold:** Steam shows a review score once a game has 10 reviews — Henry's success bar. Reviews come from purchasers (not key redeemers) — plan creator keys knowing they do not count.
