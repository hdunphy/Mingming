# Norse Expansion: Art Direction & AI Prompts

This document provides standardized AI art prompts for the **Nine Realms Pack** to ensure a cohesive "Neon Industrial" aesthetic across all MingMing sprites and Program icons.

---

## **1. The Core Aesthetic: "Neon Industrial"**
- **Style Keywords:** Industrial machinery, glowing circuitry, clean line art, high-contrast, 2D game sprite.
- **Color Palette:** Metallic grays/blacks with vibrant elemental neon (Fire = Orange-Red, Ice = Cyan, Nature = Emerald, etc.).

---

## **2. MingMing Sprite Prompts (Recommended Tool: Midjourney/Leonardo)**

### **Fire: SURTR (The Sun-Eater)**
> *A 2D game sprite of a massive mechanical robot with molten armored plates, internal glowing orange fusion core, holding a burning plasma blade, industrial terraforming design, isometric view, flat colors, clean line art, white background --v 6.0*

### **Water: JORMUNGANDR (The World-Serpent)**
> *A 2D game sprite of a long serpentine robot made of thick industrial cables and aquatic scrubbing sensors, glowing blue neon rings along its body, underwater mechanical aesthetic, isometric view, flat colors, clean line art, white background --v 6.0*

### **Earth: FAFNIR (The Vault-Guard)**
> *A 2D game sprite of a heavy-duty mining robot shaped like a dragon, gold-plated armor plating, mechanical drills for claws, glowing amber sensors, industrial bulky design, isometric view, flat colors, clean line art, white background --v 6.0*

### **Dark: HEL (The Data-Lich)**
> *A 2D game sprite of a floating humanoid server-bank robot, half-exposed circuitry, half-polished black obsidian shell, glowing purple data-leaks, ghostly industrial aesthetic, isometric view, flat colors, clean line art, white background --v 6.0*

---

## **3. Program Icon Prompts (Recommended Tool: DALL-E 3)**

Use these for the 114 card icons. Keep the aspect ratio 1:1.

### **General Template:**
> *A minimalist 2D vector game icon for a [Element] program called "[Program Name]", [Visual Action Description], glowing [Color] neon on a dark metallic background, clean flat design.*

### **Example: "Tidal Constrict" (Jormungandr)**
> *A minimalist 2D vector game icon for a Water program called "Tidal Constrict", mechanical tentacles crushing a glowing core, glowing cyan neon on a dark metallic background, clean flat design.*

### **Example: "Meltdown" (Surtr)**
> *A minimalist 2D vector game icon for a Fire program called "Meltdown", a warning symbol with a cracked glowing sun inside, glowing hot orange neon on a dark metallic background, clean flat design.*
