# Expansion: Norse Mythology - Full Card List

This document expands on the **Nine Realms Pack**, providing a full list of cards for the Norse-inspired MingMings. Each archetype contains approximately 10-15 cards, designed for Slay the Spire-style deck sizes (~30-40 cards per Developer deck).

---

## **1. SURTR (Fire)**
**Archetype A: "World-Burner" (Burn Focus)**
*Focus: Escalating Burn stages and punishing high-heat targets.*
1. **Ignite Pipeline:** Apply Burn (Stage 1). If already Burned, upgrade to Stage 2. (Cost: 1)
2. **Magma Surge:** Deal 30 damage. Target takes +5 damage from Burn for 3 turns. (Cost: 1)
3. **Heat Sink:** Remove Burn from target to gain +2 Energy. (Cost: 0)
4. **Combustion:** Deal 40 damage. If target is Stage 3 Burned, deal 80 instead. (Cost: 2)
5. **Sulfur Cloud:** Apply Stage 1 Burn to the entire enemy Side. (Cost: 2)
6. **Firebrand:** For the next 3 turns, your Attacks apply Stage 1 Burn. (Cost: 2)
7. **Inferno Core:** Deal 10 damage to all enemies for every Stage of Burn they have. (Cost: 3)
8. **Ashes to Ashes:** If an enemy faints while Burned, heal 20 HP. (Cost: 1)
9. **Superheat:** Target takes double damage from the next Burn tick. (Cost: 1)
10. **Surtr's Wrath:** Deal 100 damage. Targets with Stage 3 Burn are also Stunned. (Cost: 4)

**Archetype B: "Ragnarok" (High-Risk/Burst)**
*Focus: Sacrificing HP/Energy for overwhelming power.*
1. **Self-Immolate:** Take 15 damage, gain 2 Strengthened stacks. (Cost: 0)
2. **Nova Blast:** Deal 70 damage to all units (including allies). (Cost: 2)
3. **Critical Overload:** Your next Program costs 0 but deals 20 damage to you. (Cost: 1)
4. **Meltdown:** Deal damage equal to your missing HP. (Cost: 3)
5. **Final Ember:** If your HP is below 25%, deal 150 damage. (Cost: 2)
6. **Blazing Rebirth:** If fainted this turn, return with 10% HP and full Energy. (Cost: 4)
7. **Radiant Ruin:** Deal 50 damage. Gain 1 Energy for every 20 HP you've lost this battle. (Cost: 2)
8. **Volcanic Lunge:** Move forward and deal 90 damage. Take 10 recoil damage. (Cost: 2)

---

## **2. JORMUNGANDR (Water)**
**Archetype A: "Crushing Depths" (Stun/Control)**
*Focus: Using Dazed/Stunned to paralyze the enemy.*
1. **Venomous Coil:** Deal 20 damage and apply 2 Dazed stacks. (Cost: 1)
2. **Abyssal Grip:** If target is Dazed, apply Stunned (1 turn). (Cost: 2)
3. **Drown:** Target loses 5 HP every time they attempt to play a card while Dazed. (Cost: 2)
4. **Constrict:** Target cannot use the "Transfer Energy" action for 3 turns. (Cost: 1)
5. **Murky Water:** Apply 3 Dazed stacks to the entire enemy Side. (Cost: 3)
6. **Pressure Spike:** Deal 10 damage for every Dazed stack on the target. (Cost: 1)
7. **Suffocate:** Target's Energy max is reduced by 1 for 2 turns. (Cost: 2)

**Archetype B: "Tidal Constrict" (Energy Denial)**
*Focus: Starving the enemy Developer of programmatic resources.*
1. **Current Tug:** Target loses 1 Energy. You gain 1 Energy. (Cost: 1)
2. **Energy Leak:** Every time target plays a Program, they lose 5 HP. (Cost: 2)
3. **Vortex:** The next card the enemy Developer draws is immediately discarded. (Cost: 2)
4. **Static Sea:** Enemy Programs cost +1 Energy next turn. (Cost: 3)
5. **Depth Charge:** Deal 50 damage. Target loses all remaining Energy. (Cost: 3)
6. **Siphon Stream:** Deal 30 damage. Draw 1 card for every 2 Energy the target has. (Cost: 2)

---

## **3. FAFNIR (Earth)**
**Archetype A: "Golden Scales" (Defense/Reflect)**
*Focus: Stacking Sharp and dealing damage through resilience.*
1. **Iron Hide:** Gain 3 Sharp stacks. (Cost: 1)
2. **Spiked Plate:** For 3 turns, deal 10 damage back to any Attacker. (Cost: 2)
3. **Heavy Slam:** Deal damage equal to your Defense stat. (Cost: 2)
4. **Bulwark:** Gain 5 Sharp stacks but become Dazed (1 stack). (Cost: 1)
5. **Earthquake:** Deal 40 damage to all enemies. Power increases by 5 for every Sharp stack you have. (Cost: 3)
6. **Unshakable:** Remove all Weakened and Dazed stacks. Gain 10 HP. (Cost: 1)

**Archetype B: "Scrap Hoard" (Economy)**
*Focus: Scaling power based on the Scrap economy/Discard pile.*
1. **Recycle:** Move a card from Discard to Hand. (Cost: 1)
2. **Compactor:** Deal 30 damage. If this kills, gain 20 Scraps. (Cost: 1)
3. **Scrap Shield:** Gain Sharp stacks equal to 10% of your current Scrap count. (Cost: 2)
4. **Foundry Blast:** Deal damage equal to total cards in your Discard pile. (Cost: 3)
5. **Hoarder's Greed:** Draw 2 cards. If you have over 500 Scraps, draw 4 instead. (Cost: 1)

---

## **4. HRUESVELGR (Air)**
**Archetype A: "Hurricane Force" (Cycling)**
*Focus: Gaining power by discarding and drawing cards.*
1. **Tailwind:** Draw 2 cards, then Discard 1. (Cost: 1)
2. **Draft:** Every time you Discard a card this turn, gain 5 HP. (Cost: 1)
3. **Sonic Boom:** Deal 40 damage. If you have discarded 3+ cards this turn, deal 80. (Cost: 2)
4. **Trade Winds:** Swap your Hand with your Discard pile. (Cost: 4)
5. **Gale Force:** Deal 20 damage to all enemies. Shuffle your Discard into your Deck. (Cost: 2)

**Archetype B: "Wind-Chill" (Ice Synergy)**
1. **Frost-Bite:** Deal 30 damage and apply Ice-vulnerability (Next Ice attack deals 1.5x). (Cost: 1)
2. **North Wind:** Change your primary Element to Ice for the next 2 turns. (Cost: 2)
3. **Hypothermia:** Target loses 1 Energy for every Ice-type Program in your Discard pile. (Cost: 2)

---

## **5. RATATOSKR (Nature)**
**Archetype A: "Echo-Chamber" (Multi-Cast)**
1. **Double-Tap:** Your next Program triggers twice. (Cost: 2)
2. **Acorn Shot:** Deal 10 damage 3 times. (Cost: 1)
3. **Chatter:** Apply 1 Weakened stack 3 times to a target. (Cost: 2)
4. **Recursive Loop:** If the last card played was Nature, return it to Hand. (Cost: 1)

**Archetype B: "Grafting" (Buff Transfer)**
1. **Branch Out:** Move all Strengthened stacks from Self to an Ally. (Cost: 1)
2. **Pollinate:** Copy all Sharp stacks from an Ally to Self. (Cost: 2)
3. **Symbiosis:** For 3 turns, when an Ally heals, you heal for 50%. (Cost: 2)

---

## **6. YMIR (Ice)**
**Archetype A: "Permafrost" (Duration)**
1. **Deep Freeze:** Existing Status Effects on the target do not decrement next turn. (Cost: 2)
2. **Glacial Age:** All enemies are Asleep for 1 turn. (Cost: 4)
3. **Frostbite:** Deal 10 damage. Increase the duration of Burn or Poison on target by 1. (Cost: 1)

**Archetype B: "Brittle Point" (Shatter)**
1. **Ice Smash:** Deal 40 damage. If target is Asleep, deal 120. (Cost: 2)
2. **Shatter Code:** Deal 60 damage. If target is Stunned, reduce their Defense by 10 permanently. (Cost: 3)

---

## **7. HEIMDALL (Light)**
**Archetype A: "Prismatic Shield" (Reflection)**
1. **Mirror Image:** Reflect the next damaging Program back at the attacker. (Cost: 2)
2. **Light Barrier:** Gain 50 temporary HP that lasts for 1 turn. (Cost: 2)
3. **Prism Beam:** Deal 40 damage. If a Shield was broken this turn, deal 80. (Cost: 2)

**Archetype B: "Gjallarhorn" (Team Buffs)**
1. **Resonate:** All allied MingMings gain 2 Strengthened stacks. (Cost: 3)
2. **Bifrost Call:** One allied MingMing gains full Energy. (Cost: 4)
3. **Radiant Hope:** Heal all allied MingMings for 15 HP. (Cost: 2)

## **8. HEL (Dark)**
**Archetype A: "Necrotic Code" (Drain)**
1. **Soul Siphon:** Deal 30 damage, heal for 15. (Cost: 1)
2. **Wither:** Target's Attack and Defense are reduced by 5% for the rest of battle. (Cost: 3)
3. **Death Grip:** Deal 50 damage. If target is below 20% HP, heal to full. (Cost: 4)

**Archetype B: "Grave-Call" (Discard/Revive)**
1. **Corrupted File:** Play the top card of your Discard pile for 0 cost. (Cost: 2)
2. **Reboot:** Revive a fainted MingMing with 1 HP. (Cost: 5)
3. **Data Ghost:** Deal 10 damage for every fainted MingMing in your party. (Cost: 1)
