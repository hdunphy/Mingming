# Expansion: Norse Mythology - The Nine Realms Pack

This document defines sixteen MingMings inspired by Norse mythology, each representing one of the core elements. Each MingMing comes with two distinct OS (Operating System) archetypes designed to integrate into the existing combat engine.

---

## **1. Fire Element (Focus: Aggression & Burn)**

### **Fenrir (The Monstrous Wolf)**
*Narrative:* A wolf-like robot intended for a multitude of purposes. Excels at carrying and increasing its own strength.
- **OS v1: `UNBOUND_KERNEL` (Glass Cannon):** Fenrir starts every battle with 3 stacks of "Strengthened," but takes 2% of his max HP as recoil damage every time he plays a Fire Attack card.
- **OS v2: `CINDER_WALL_OS` (Defensive Burn):** Whenever Fenrir applies the "Burn" status to an enemy, he gains a stack of Sharp. This allows Fenrir to be played as a defensive control tank rather than an all-out attacker.

### **Sköll (The Sun-Chaser)**
*Narrative:* A high-speed interceptor unit designed to pursue heat signatures.
- **OS v1: `TREACHERY_KERNEL` (Selfish Aggro):** Whenever an allied Mingming takes damage from an enemy attack, Sköll gains 1 stack of "Strengthened".
- **OS v2: `SOLAR_FLARE_OS` (Combo Burst):** Fire cards cost 1 less Energy if the target is currently suffering from 3rd Degree Burn.

---

## **2. Water Element (Focus: Control & Energy Manipulation)**

### **Kraken (The Deep Sea Terror)**
*Narrative:* An underwater monstrosity that controls the very ecosystem of the deep seas. This is the true god of the seas.
- **OS v1: `ABYSSAL_INK_SYS` (Disruption):** Whenever you draw a card outside of your normal Turn Phase, apply 1 stack of "Dazed" to a random enemy.
- **OS v2: `TIDAL_CRUSH_OS` (Big Spells):** Water cards that cost 3 or more Energy deal 30% more damage and cannot be dodged or blocked.

### **Jörmungandr (The World Serpent)**
*Narrative:* A deep-sea atmospheric scrubber that grew so large its cable-body encircles entire oceanic sectors.
- **OS v1: `OUROBOROS_LOOP` (Card Spam):** If you play 3 or more Water cards in a single turn, instantly gain 1 Energy and draw 1 card.
- **OS v2: `VENOM_TRENCH_OS` (Stall):** Jörmungandr heals for 2 HP at the end of the turn for every stack of "Poisoned" currently on the enemy field.

---

## **3. Earth Element (Focus: Defense & Transformation)**

### **Fafnir (The Cursed Dragon)**
*Narrative:* A heavy-duty mining robot that developed a glitched obsession with "Scrap" accumulation.
- **OS v1: `HOARD_PROTOCOL` (Combo Preparation):** Unspent Energy is retained between turns, but Fafnir takes 1% maximum HP damage per hoarded Energy at the start of his turn.
- **OS v2: `CORRUPTED_GOLD_OS` (Self-Sabotage):** Fafnir gains 1 Energy whenever a negative status effect is applied to him.

### **Gullinbursti (The Golden Boar)**
*Narrative:* A gilded heavy-impact unit that gains momentum through defensive maneuvers.
- **OS v1: `UNSTOPPABLE_MASS` (Defense Weaving):** Playing a defensive Status card reduces the Energy cost of the next Attack card you play this turn by 1.
- **OS v2: `KINETIC_RAM_OS` (Defense-to-Offense):** Earth Attack cards deal additional damage equal to Gullinbursti's current stacks of "Sharp".

---

## **4. Air Element (Focus: Card Draw & Evasion)**

### **Hræsvelgr (The Corpse-Swallower)**
*Narrative:* A high-altitude wind-turbine robot that generates storms with its massive solar-sail wings.
- **OS v1: `GALE_FORCE_OS` (Discard Synergy):** Whenever you voluntarily discard a card from your hand, deal moderate Air damage to a random enemy.
- **OS v2: `UPDRAFT_KERNEL` (Energy Ramp):** The first time you cycle through your entire deck and shuffle your discard pile, permanently gain +1 max Energy for the rest of the battle.

### **Sleipnir (The Eight-Legged Steed)**
*Narrative:* A multi-legged tactical transport designed for high-frequency engagement.
- **OS v1: `MOMENTUM_DRIVE` (Speed & Strength):** Whenever you play a card that costs 0 Energy, Sleipnir gains 1 stack of "Strengthened".
- **OS v2: `WAR_STEED_OS` (Card Generation):** Whenever Sleipnir plays an Air Attack card, generate a 0-cost "Hoof Strike" token card in your hand that disappears after use.

---

## **5. Nature Element (Focus: Status Cleansing & Ramp)**

### **Ratatoskr (The World-Tree Squirrel)**
*Narrative:* A nimble, arboreal networking bot designed to bridge communications between planetary sectors.
- **OS v1: `GOSSIP_NODE` (Zero-Cost Spam):** Whenever you play a card that costs 0 Energy, all of your active Mingmings heal 1 HP.
- **OS v2: `INSTIGATOR_OS` (Debuff Weaving):** Whenever Ratatoskr plays a 0-cost card, he applies 1 stack of "Dazed" to the target.

### **Huldra (The Forest Spirit)**
*Narrative:* A stealth-focused environmental monitoring unit with organic mimicry armor.
- **OS v1: `ALLURE_PROXY` (Debuff Spreader):** Whenever Huldra applies a buff to herself or an ally, she mirrors it by applying 1 stack of "Weakened" or "Dazed" to a random enemy.
- **OS v2: `BARK_SHIELD_OS` (Early Protection):** Huldra starts every battle with a massive, temporary shield that degrades by 20% every turn.

---

## **6. Ice Element (Focus: Lockdown & HP Taxing)**

### **Ymir (The Frost Giant)**
*Narrative:* A cryo-stasis unit that went rogue, attempting to preserve the entire planet in an eternal winter.
- **OS v1: `RIME_HEART_SYS` (Punish Attackers):** Enemies that deal damage to the Jötunn automatically receive 1 stack of "Weakened".
- **OS v2: `GLACIAL_PACE_OS` (Heavy Hitter):** Jötunn cannot play more than 2 cards per turn, but all Ice cards deal 50% more base damage.

### **Draugr (The Undead Warrior)**
*Narrative:* A decommissioned combat frame reanimated by corrupted, frozen sub-routines.
- **OS v1: `PERMAFROST_WAKE` (Sleep Synergy):** Whenever a Draugr recovers from the "Asleep" status or is revived, it gains 3 strength.
- **OS v2: `GRAVE_CHILL_OS` (Energy Tax):** Enemies afflicted with 2 or more negative status effects cost 1 additional Energy to execute their attacks against the Draugr.

---

## **7. Light Element (Focus: Healing & Buffs)**

### **Valkyrie (Chooser of the Slain)**
*Narrative:* A high-frequency laser defense array designed to protect the planetary FTL gates.
- **OS v1: `VALHALLA_UPLINK` (Sustain):** Whenever a Valkyrie applies a positive status (like Sharp or Strengthened) to an ally, that ally also heals for 5% of their max HP.
- **OS v2: `EINHERJAR_RALLY` (Party Synergy):** For every other active Mingming currently on your side of the field, Valkyrie's Light attacks deal +10% damage.

### **Audhumbla (The Primeval Cow)**
*Narrative:* A massive resource-generator designed to sustain deep-space colonies.
- **OS v1: `GENESIS_FIRMWARE` (Scaling Utility):** Every 3rd Heal or Spell card played permanently increases Audhumbla's maximum Energy pool by 1 for the remainder of the battle.
- **OS v2: `NOURISH_ROUTINE` (Overheal):** Any healing applied to Audhumbla that exceeds her maximum HP is automatically converted into an offensive Light attack against a random enemy.

---

## **8. Dark Element (Focus: Poison & High-Risk)**

### **Hel (Ruler of the Underworld)**
*Narrative:* A corrupted server bank that stores the "Architectures" of decommissioned and fainted MingMings.
- **OS v1: `EQUINOX_TOGGLE` (Stance Switcher):** Hel shifts stances based on the cards she plays (Heal = Light Stance, Attack = Dark Stance).
- **OS v2: `UNDERWORLD_GATEWAY` (HP Manipulation):** Hel's Dark spells cost HP instead of Energy.

### **Níðhöggr (The Corpse Devourer)**
*Narrative:* A corrupted terraforming drill that gnaws at the roots of the planetary network.
- **OS v1: `ROOT_CORRUPTION` (Deep Poison):** Poison applied by Níðhöggr does not decrease in stacks at the end of the turn.
- **OS v2: `FALLEN_FEAST_OS` (Snowball):** Whenever any Mingming is defeated, Níðhöggr gains 3 stacks of "Strengthened" and 3 stacks of "Sharp".
