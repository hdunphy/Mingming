# Expansion: Norse Mythology - The Nine Realms Pack

This document defines eight new MingMings inspired by Norse mythology, each representing one of the core elements. Each MingMing comes with two distinct card-based archetypes (15-20 cards total per MingMing) designed to integrate into the existing combat engine.

---

## **1. Fire: SURTR (The Sun-Eater)**
*Narrative:* A titanic AI core encased in molten terraforming plates, originally designed to reignite dying stars.
- **Archetype A: "World-Burner" (Aggro/Burn Focus)**
    - Focuses on rapidly escalating the **Burn** status to Stage 3 and punishing already-burned targets.
- **Archetype B: "Ragnarok" (High-Risk/Burst Focus)**
    - Uses self-damaging programs to unleash massive, unblockable AOE strikes.

## **2. Water: JORMUNGANDR (The World-Serpent)**
*Narrative:* A deep-sea atmospheric scrubber that grew so large its cable-body encircles entire oceanic sectors.
- **Archetype A: "Crushing Depths" (Stun/Control Focus)**
    - Uses the **Stunned** and **Dazed** status to lock down enemy actions while dealing chip damage.
- **Archetype B: "Tidal Constrict" (Energy Denial Focus)**
    - Programs that increase the energy cost of enemy cards or drain their energy directly.

## **3. Earth: FAFNIR (The Vault-Guard)**
*Narrative:* A heavy-duty mining robot that developed a glitched obsession with "Scrap" accumulation.
- **Archetype A: "Golden Scales" (Defense/Sharp Focus)**
    - Maximizes the **Sharp** status to become nearly immune to physical attacks, reflecting damage back.
- **Archetype B: "Scrap Hoard" (Economy/Resource Focus)**
    - Gains power based on the number of cards in the discard pile or the amount of Scrap in the Developer's inventory.

## **4. Air: HRUESVELGR (The Corpse-Swallower)**
*Narrative:* A high-altitude wind-turbine robot that generates storms with its massive solar-sail wings.
- **Archetype A: "Hurricane Force" (Shuffle/Discard Focus)**
    - Forces the Developer to discard and redraw cards, triggering effects every time a card is cycled.
- **Archetype B: "Wind-Chill" (Ice Synergy Focus)**
    - Air-type programs that apply the **Ice** element vulnerability, preparing targets for heavy Ice attacks.

## **5. Nature: RATATOSKR (The Messenger)**
*Narrative:* A nimble, arboreal networking bot designed to bridge communications between planetary sectors.
- **Archetype A: "Echo-Chamber" (Multi-Cast Focus)**
    - Lower-power programs that trigger 2-3 times sequentially, maximizing the impact of **Weakened** or **Poisoned** stacks.
- **Archetype B: "Grafting" (Buff-Transfer Focus)**
    - Allows the Developer to move status effects (like **Strengthened**) between allied MingMings without using the Transfer Energy action.

## **6. Ice: YMIR (The First-Freeze)**
*Narrative:* A cryo-stasis unit that went rogue, attempting to preserve the entire planet in an eternal winter.
- **Archetype A: "Permafrost" (Duration Focus)**
    - Increases the duration of existing status effects (like **Asleep** or **Stunned**) so they don't decrement during the Pre-Turn phase.
- **Archetype B: "Brittle Point" (Shatter Focus)**
    - Deals massive bonus damage to targets that are currently under a CC status effect (Asleep/Stunned).

## **7. Light: HEIMDALL (The Bifrost-Sentry)**
*Narrative:* A high-frequency laser defense array designed to protect the planetary FTL gates.
- **Archetype A: "Prismatic Shield" (Reactive Defense)**
    - Creates "Light Barriers" that intercept the next 1-2 programs played by the enemy, reflecting them back.
- **Archetype B: "Gjallarhorn" (Team-Buff Focus)**
    - Massive, expensive programs that apply **Strengthened** and **Sharp** to the entire allied side simultaneously.

## **8. Dark: HEL (The Data-Lich)**
*Narrative:* A corrupted server bank that stores the "Architectures" of decommissioned and fainted MingMings.
- **Archetype A: "Necrotic Code" (Lifesteal/Drain Focus)**
    - Programs that heal the source MingMing for 50% of the damage dealt to the target.
- **Archetype B: "Grave-Call" (Discard/Revive Focus)**
    - Gains power based on fainted allies or allows the temporary use of "Ghost Programs" from the discard pile.
