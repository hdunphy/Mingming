## 1. The quick version

You lead a team of **Mingmings** (robot monsters from Norse myth) across a map of fights. Every fight is a card battle. Win fights to earn **Amber**, new cards and **Traces**. Spend Amber at Markets, grow your team at Dens, and beat the gym leader at the end of the map to clear the run.

Then do it again with a different team.

**The loop**
- **Fight** wild Mingmings and rivals
- **Pick** a new card after every win
- **Shop** at Markets and **recruit** at Dens
- **Beat the leader** at the end of the map
- **Keep your Traces** and start a new run

## 2. Your first run

Pick a starter:
- **Kraken** (Water)
- **Fenrir** (Fire)
- **Ratatoskr** (Nature)

Your first run is a short guided one called the **intro run**. It's a small hand-built map: a few wild fights, a free recruit (one of the two starters you didn't pick), a Market stop, and a leader fight at the end. Win or lose, it ends the same way, and it doesn't count toward your real progress. Then the full game opens up.

Two switches on the starter screen (and in Settings):
- **Skip intro** jumps straight to a full run. Saved per save file.
- **Show advanced content** brings back the panels that stay hidden until you have something to put in them (Draughts, Runes, Totems, tiers).

Small tips pop up as toasts during play. Press **Hide tips** on one to turn them off.

## 3. How a fight works

You bring up to **3 Mingmings** against up to 3 enemies. Defeat every enemy to win. HP is fully restored after every fight, so damage never carries forward.

**One shared deck.** Mingmings don't own cards. Every card from your whole team goes into one deck, and you play from one shared hand (up to 15 cards). The more Mingmings you have standing, the more cards you draw each turn.

**Energy belongs to each Mingming.** Most have 2. Playing a card spends Energy from whoever casts it. Energy refills every turn and never carries over, so spend it. A Mingming at 0 HP can't act.

**Your turn**
1. Pick a card in your hand
2. Pick who casts it (click their plate). This matters for Energy and STAB
3. Pick a target
4. Cast it. Repeat until you're out of Energy, then **End Turn**

The enemy then plays its own cards. You can open the **combat log** and the **enemy's hand** from the battle screen. Both start closed.

## 4. Elements and STAB

This build has three elements that beat each other in a loop:

**Fire beats Nature. Nature beats Water. Water beats Fire.**

Neutral cards have no matchups and no STAB.

- **Super effective:** an attack on an element it beats deals ×1.5 damage.
- **STAB** (Same Type Attack Bonus): a card whose element matches its caster's element deals ×1.5. A Fire card cast by Fenrir gets it. The same card cast by Kraken doesn't.
- They stack: STAB plus super effective is ×2.25.

Each element has its own colour and symbol, so you're never relying on colour alone. Hover an element badge to see the matchups.

That's why **who casts the card** matters. Same card, same target, different caster, different damage.

## 5. Controls

Mouse works for everything, and you can drag a card onto its target. The keyboard path:

```
1-9           Select a card (left to right)
W / E / R     Select a caster (your party, in order)
Shift+W/E/R   Target an ally (heals and buffs)
A / S / D     Target an enemy
Tab           Cycle enemies (Shift+Tab goes back)
Enter         Cast
Z / X / C     Fire a Draught
Space         End turn
Esc           Clear selection (or open Settings)
```

Volume, theme and **Abandon run** live in Settings.

## 6. The map

A run is a map split into three biomes, ending at the gym. Every node shows what's inside it one layer ahead, so each step is a real choice.

**What you'll find on the map**
- **Wild:** the ordinary fight
- **Rival:** a wild fight with a different element from the one you'd expect. Beat it for its Trace
- **Elite:** one harder fight with a Totem as the prize
- **Alpha:** one overtuned wild Mingming against your whole team. It guards a rare Trace
- **Ambush:** their 3 against your 2. High risk
- **Market:** shop
- **Den:** recruit and retrain
- **Event:** a short choice with a reward or a cost. Most can be left alone for free
- **Scout:** the last fight before the gym, which previews the leader's team
- **Gym:** the leader's gauntlet of three fights in a row

## 7. Between fights

After every win you pick **one new card** for your shared deck (or skip), and you earn **Amber**. Wild Mingmings sometimes drop a **Trace**.

**Market**
- Buy cards and Draughts
- Sell cards you don't want
- Pay Amber to remove junk, like Corrupted Data (a 1-Energy card that does nothing)
- Upgrade cards, and slot Runes

**Den**
- **Summon** a new Mingming from a Trace and add it to your party (up to 3)
- **Retrain** a Mingming to a different Instinct
- Upgrade cards

**Events** trigger once, on your first visit. A few cost you something, and the game shows you the odds on any gamble.

Each new Mingming brings a 5-card kit into the shared deck. You can't have the same species twice unless they're on different Instincts.

## 8. Building your team

Four things shape how your team plays beyond its cards:

- **Instinct:** a Mingming's built-in passive rule. Every species has two to choose from when you Summon. Many show a little counter on the plate (like 2/5) so you can see progress.
- **Rune:** a rider that tweaks an Instinct. One per Mingming. The game shows what a Rune does on that exact Mingming, and never offers one that would do nothing.
- **Totem:** a passive for your whole team. Enemy leaders carry them too.
- **Draught:** a single-use item with 3 slots. It fires for free (no Energy) on your turn, with Z / X / C.

Cards can also be **Auras**: play one and it stays active on that Mingming for the rest of the fight.

Most of this stays hidden until you have something to put in it. **Show advanced content** brings it all back at once.

## 9. Winning, losing and what you keep

**Win the run** by beating the gym leader's three-fight gauntlet. **Lose the run** if your whole team goes down.

Either way, **Traces are what you keep**. They're how you Summon new Mingmings in later runs. The **Codex** records every card, Mingming and Instinct you've met. It's a collection, and nothing in it makes you stronger.

**Tiers and modifiers** unlock after you clear a gym:
- **Tier 0 Standard:** the game as it is
- **Tier 1 Armed Wilds:** wild Mingmings run their Instincts
- **Tier 2 Elite Territory:** one more elite per biome, and wilds play smarter
- **Tier 3 Leaders' Drivers:** the leader's Totem is active in all three gauntlet fights

Optional **modifiers** only change the rules, never the stats: **Junk Start**, **Tight Budget** (shop prices +25%), **Elite Hunt**, **No Recruits**, **Draft Start**.

## 10. Dictionary (A to H)

**Alpha:** an overtuned wild Mingming that guards a rare Trace.
**Ambush:** a risky fight, their 3 against your 2.
**Amber:** the currency. Earned from fights, spent at Markets.
**Aura:** a card that installs on a Mingming for the rest of the fight.
**Asleep:** can't act. Starts at 3 stacks. Each turn and each hit removes one, so three hits wake it.
**Bark Shield:** absorbs damage before HP. Fades 20% each turn.
**Biome:** one third of a map. A run crosses three.
**Burn:** damage over time at the start of its owner's turn. Gets stronger from 1.5% to 8% of max HP at 1 to 4 stacks and never fades. Past 4 stacks it detonates for 14% of max HP.
**Card:** an action you play from your shared hand.
**Caster:** the Mingming you choose to play a card.
**Codex:** a record of everything you've met. Gives no power.
**Dazed:** takes more damage per stack. Cancelled by Sharp.
**Den:** where you Summon, Retrain and upgrade cards.
**Draught:** single-use item. 3 slots, free to fire.
**Elite:** one harder fight that carries a Totem.
**Energized:** +1 Energy per stack at the next refill.
**Energy:** each Mingming's per-turn pool for playing cards. Refills every turn.
**Event:** a one-time choice node.
**Exhaust:** the card leaves the fight after playing. No reshuffle.

## 10. Dictionary (G to R)

**Gauntlet:** the gym's three fights in a row.
**Gym:** the final node of a run, held by a leader.
**Hand:** the cards you can play this turn. Shared by the whole team, max 15.
**Instinct:** a Mingming's passive rule. Two per species.
**Junk:** a card that does nothing, like Corrupted Data. Pay Amber at a Market to remove it.
**Leader:** the gym's boss.
**Market:** a shop for cards, Draughts, upgrades and Runes.
**Mingming:** the robot monsters. The plural is Mingmings too.
**Modifier:** an optional rule twist for a run, unlocked after your first gym clear.
**Neutral:** a card with no element. No matchups, no STAB.
**Poison:** damage at the start of its owner's turn, 1% of max HP per stack, then loses 1 stack.
**Regen:** heals 2% of max HP at the start of its owner's turn, then loses 1 stack.
**Retrain:** swap a Mingming to its other Instinct.
**Rival:** a wild fight with the other element on the road. Beat it for its Trace.
**Rune:** a rider that tweaks an Instinct. One per Mingming.
**Run:** one trip from the first node to the gym.

## 10. Dictionary (S to Z)

**Scout:** the last fight before the gym. It previews the leader's team.
**Shared deck:** every card from your whole team, in one deck.
**Sharp:** takes less damage per stack. Cancelled by Dazed.
**STAB:** Same Type Attack Bonus. Card element matches caster element: ×1.5 damage.
**Stack:** a count on a status. More stacks, bigger effect.
**StableOS:** brief immunity to Asleep and Stunned, granted right after waking or recovering.
**Starter:** the Mingming you begin with. Kraken, Fenrir or Ratatoskr.
**Strengthened:** deals more damage per stack. Cancelled by Weakened.
**Stunned:** skips its turn. Doesn't stack.
**Summon:** turn a Trace into a new Mingming at a Den.
**Super effective:** an attack on an element it beats. ×1.5.
**Tier:** a harder version of the game, unlocked by clearing gyms.
**Token:** a temporary card created during a fight.
**Totem:** a passive for the whole team. Enemy leaders have them too.
**Trace:** a drop from fights. Keep it between runs. Used to Summon a Mingming.
**Weakened:** deals less damage per stack. Cancelled by Strengthened.
