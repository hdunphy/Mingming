# Mingming: Midgard Circuit — How to Play

Thanks for testing! Mingming is a Norse robot monster-collecting roguelike deckbuilder. Recruit monsters. Build combos. Break the game.

This is an early build. The art is placeholder, the numbers are still moving, and bugs are expected. Finding them is why you're here.

## 1. The quick version

You lead a team of Mingmings (robot monsters from Norse myth) along a map of fights. Every fight is a card battle. Win fights to earn Amber, new cards and Traces. Spend Amber in towns, grow your team at a Den, and beat the gym leader at the end of the map. Then start a new run with what you learned.

- Fight your way along the routes
- Pick a new card after every win
- Shop and recruit in towns
- Beat the leader at the end of the map
- Keep your Traces and go again

## 2. Your first run

Pick a starter: Kraken (Water), Fenrir (Fire) or Ratatoskr (Nature).

Your first run is a short guided one called the intro run. It's a small hand-built map: a few wild fights, a free recruit (one of the two starters you didn't pick), a shop stop, and a leader fight at the end. Win or lose it ends the same way, and it doesn't count toward your real progress. Then the full game opens up.

Two switches on the starter screen, and in Settings:

- **Skip intro:** jump straight to a full run. Saved per save file.
- **Show advanced content:** brings back panels that stay hidden until you have something to put in them (Draughts, Runes, Totems, tiers).

Short tips pop up as toasts. Press Hide tips on one to turn them off.

## 3. How a fight works

You bring up to 3 Mingmings against up to 3 enemies. Defeat every enemy to win. HP is fully restored after every fight, so damage never carries forward.

One shared deck. Mingmings don't own cards. Every card from your whole team goes into one deck, and you play from one shared hand (up to 15 cards). The more Mingmings you have standing, the more cards you draw each turn.

Energy belongs to each Mingming. Most have 2. Playing a card spends Energy from whoever casts it. Energy refills every turn and never carries over, so spend it. A Mingming at 0 HP can't act.

Your turn:

1. Pick a card in your hand
2. Pick who casts it (click their plate). This matters for Energy and STAB
3. Pick a target
4. Cast it. Repeat until you're out of Energy, then End Turn

The enemy then plays its own cards. You can open the combat log and the enemy's hand from the battle screen. Both start closed.

## 4. Elements and STAB

This build has three elements that beat each other in a loop:

Fire beats Nature. Nature beats Water. Water beats Fire.

Neutral cards have no matchups and no STAB.

- **Super effective:** an attack on an element it beats deals ×1.5 damage.
- **STAB (Same Type Attack Bonus):** a card whose element matches its caster's element deals ×1.5. A Fire card cast by Fenrir gets it. The same card cast by Kraken doesn't.
- **They stack:** STAB plus super effective is ×2.25.

Every element has a colour and a symbol, so you're never relying on colour alone. Hover an element badge to see the matchups.

That's why who casts a card matters. Same card, same target, different caster, different damage.

## 5. Controls

Mouse works for everything, and you can drag a card onto its target. The keyboard path:

```
1-9              Select a card (left to right)
W / E / R        Select a caster (your party, in order)
Left Shift+W/E/R Target an ally (heals and buffs)
A / S / D        Target an enemy
Tab              Cycle enemies (Left Shift+Tab goes back)
Enter            Cast
Z / X / C        Fire a Draught
Space            End turn
Right Shift      Hold to fast-forward
Esc              Clear selection (or open Settings)
```

Settings also has Battle speed: Slow, Showy (the default), Snappy, Fast and Instant. Volume, theme and Abandon run are there too.

## 6. The map

A run crosses three biomes. Each biome is a stretch of numbered routes joined by one town, and it ends at a gate: an Elite at the end of the first two, and the gym at the end of the third.

- Travel is one-way. You only move forward, and every node is visited once.
- Routes split and merge, so you choose your path.
- Every node's type is visible from the start. Which Mingmings you'll meet stays hidden until you scout. Ping Sweep (a Draught) and the Relay Tower event can reveal them.
- Each biome has one optional detour, a side branch that costs one extra fight.
- A new run drops you straight into the first fight.

Expect about 11 or 12 fights on the way, then the gym's three-fight gauntlet.

What you'll find:

- **Wild:** the ordinary fight
- **Rival:** a wild fight with the other element on this road. Beat it for its Trace
- **Elite:** one harder fight, with a Totem as the prize
- **Alpha (detour):** one overtuned wild Mingming against your whole team, guarding a rare Trace
- **Ambush (detour):** their 3 against your 2. High risk
- **Event:** a short choice with a reward or a cost. Most can be skipped for free
- **Scout:** the last fight before the gym, which previews the leader's team
- **Town:** shop, upgrades, Den and loadout (see below)
- **Gym:** the leader's gauntlet of three fights in a row

## 7. Towns

Each town is one stop with four buildings. You can switch between them freely until you leave.

- **Shop:** buy and sell cards, Draughts and Runes. Shops can also sell a Trace. The shelf is set the first time you open the Shop tab. Pay Amber to remove junk, like Corrupted Data (a 1-Energy card that does nothing).
- **Upgrades:** upgrade cards. You get 2 upgrades per town in the first biome, 3 in the second and 4 in the third, so save Amber for the late run.
- **Den:** Summon a new Mingming from a Trace, Retrain a Mingming to a different Instinct, and manage your party and bench.
- **Loadout:** edit your team and deck.

After every fight you also pick one new card for your shared deck (or skip). The offers lean toward cards that work with your team's Instincts, and you won't see the same card twice in a row.

## 8. Building your team

Beyond its cards, a team is shaped by a few things:

- **Instinct:** a Mingming's built-in passive rule. Every species has two to choose from when you Summon. Many show a little counter on the plate (like 2/5) so you can watch progress.
- **Rune:** a rider that tweaks an Instinct. One per Mingming. The game shows what a Rune does on that exact Mingming, and never offers one that would do nothing.
- **Totem:** a passive for your whole team. Enemy leaders carry them too.
- **Draught:** a single-use item. You have 3 slots. It fires for free (no Energy) with Z / X / C.
- **Aura:** a card that stays active on its Mingming for the rest of the fight.

The six Runes are named after Norse runes:

- **Fehu:** your Instinct's number goes up (one more stack, or half again as much)
- **Jera:** an Instinct that is limited to once a turn gets one more
- **Mannaz:** an Instinct that watches only you now watches your whole side
- **Gebo:** what your Instinct gives you, the rest of your side gets too
- **Uruz:** every stack you hold counts for one more when a card cashes it in
- **Algiz:** whatever your Instinct costs you, it stops costing

Most of this stays hidden until you have something to put in it. Show advanced content brings it all back at once.

## 9. Winning, losing, what you keep

Win the run by beating the gym leader's gauntlet. Lose the run if your whole team goes down.

Either way, Traces are what you keep. They're how you Summon new Mingmings, and you can do that from the Ranch between runs as well as at a Den. Totems end with the run that won them.

The Ranch is your hub between runs. Its tabs are Expedition (start a run), Roster (pick your party), Summon, Vault (the Totems you hold) and Codex. The Codex records every card, Mingming and Instinct you've met. It's a collection, and nothing in it makes you stronger.

Clearing a gym unlocks Tiers and run modifiers:

- **Tier 0 Standard:** the game as it is
- **Tier 1 Armed Wilds:** wild Mingmings run their Instincts
- **Tier 2 Elite Territory:** one more Elite per biome, and wilds play smarter
- **Tier 3 Leaders' Totems:** the leader's Totem is active in all three gauntlet fights

Modifiers are optional rule twists. They change the rules, never the stats: Junk Start, Tight Budget (shop prices +25%), Elite Hunt, No Recruits and Draft Start.

## 10. Dictionary (1 of 3)

Statuses show as an icon with a number (stacks). Hover one for its exact rule.

**Alpha**: an overtuned wild Mingming on a detour. Guards a rare Trace.
**Ambush**: a risky detour fight, their 3 against your 2.
**Amber**: the currency. Earned from fights, spent in towns.
**Aura**: a card that stays active on its Mingming for the rest of the fight.
**Asleep**: can't act. Starts at 3 stacks. Each turn and each hit removes one, so three hits wake it.
**Bark Shield**: absorbs damage before HP. Fades 20% each turn.
**Biome**: one third of a map. A run crosses three.
**Burn**: damage at the start of its owner's turn. Grows from 1.5% to 8% of max HP at 1 to 4 stacks and never fades. Past 4 stacks it detonates for 14% of max HP.
**Card**: an action you play from your shared hand.
**Caster**: the Mingming you choose to play a card.
**Codex**: a record of everything you've met. Gives no power.
**Dazed**: takes more damage per stack. Cancelled by Sharp.
**Den**: where you Summon, Retrain and manage your party.
**Detour**: an optional side branch on a route. One extra fight.
**Draught**: a single-use item. 3 slots, free to fire.
**Elite**: one harder fight that carries a Totem.
**Energized**: +1 Energy per stack at the next refill.
**Energy**: each Mingming's per-turn pool for playing cards. Refills every turn.
**Event**: a one-time choice node.
**Exhaust**: the card leaves the fight after playing. No reshuffle.

## 10. Dictionary (2 of 3)

**Gauntlet**: the gym's three fights in a row.
**Gym**: the final node of a run, held by a leader.
**Hand**: the cards you can play this turn. Shared by the whole team, max 15.
**Instinct**: a Mingming's passive rule. Two per species.
**Junk**: a card that does nothing, like Corrupted Data. Pay Amber in a Shop to remove it.
**Leader**: the gym's boss.
**Mingming**: the robot monsters. The plural is Mingmings too.
**Modifier**: an optional rule twist for a run, unlocked after your first gym clear.
**Neutral**: a card with no element. No matchups, no STAB.
**Poison**: damage at the start of its owner's turn, 1% of max HP per stack, then loses 1 stack.
**Ranch**: your hub between runs.
**Regen**: heals 2% of max HP at the start of its owner's turn, then loses 1 stack.
**Retrain**: swap a Mingming to its other Instinct.
**Rival**: a wild fight with the other element on the road. Beat it for its Trace.
**Route**: a numbered stretch of the map between towns.
**Rune**: a rider that tweaks an Instinct. One per Mingming.
**Run**: one trip from the first fight to the gym.
**Scout**: the last fight before the gym. It previews the leader's team.
**Shared deck**: every card from your whole team, in one deck.
**Sharp**: takes less damage per stack. Cancelled by Dazed.

## 10. Dictionary (3 of 3)

**STAB**: Same Type Attack Bonus. Card element matches caster element: ×1.5 damage.
**Stack**: a count on a status. More stacks, bigger effect.
**StableOS**: brief immunity to Asleep and Stunned, granted right after waking or recovering.
**Starter**: the Mingming you begin with. Kraken, Fenrir or Ratatoskr.
**Strengthened**: deals more damage per stack. Cancelled by Weakened.
**Stunned**: skips its turn. Doesn't stack.
**Summon**: turn a Trace into a new Mingming, at a Den or the Ranch.
**Super effective**: an attack on an element it beats. ×1.5.
**Tier**: a harder version of the game, unlocked by clearing gyms.
**Token**: a temporary card created during a fight.
**Totem**: a passive for the whole team. Enemy leaders have them too. Yours end with the run.
**Town**: one stop per biome with a Shop, Upgrades, a Den and Loadout.
**Trace**: a drop from fights. Keep it between runs. Used to Summon a Mingming.
**Weakened**: deals less damage per stack. Cancelled by Strengthened.
